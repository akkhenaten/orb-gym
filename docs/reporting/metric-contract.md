## Common report envelope

Every result identifies report/metric definition version, active company, selected authorised gym set, period/cutoff, report timezone, date basis, currency/tax display basis where relevant, filter state, source provenance, snapshot/refresh times and completeness. Period intervals are start-inclusive/end-exclusive. Resolve local calendar dates in the chosen company/report timezone into explicit instants; do not pretend every day lasts24hours. A cross-gym report selects one report timezone while source schedules retain their gym timezone. Compare like-for-like periods and definitions, not current partial month against full prior month without explanation.

A period flow and point-in-time snapshot are different. Charge/refund/payment events use their documented effective dates; membership/stock/balances use as-of cutoff. Class/PT reports specify occurrence/service cohort and status-review cutoff. Forecast and future published rota are labelled planned, never actual. Actual staff/downtime/availability intervals are clipped to the period; breaks are intersected explicitly. No unchosen minute-rounding policy is silently applied.

Historical results require source status/version/effective-time history. If the runtime only has current-state rows, mark historical result unavailable or current reconstruction with limitations, never fabricate historic state. Display both source-effective time and ingestion freshness when late events matter. Corrections produce restated definition/snapshot versions with audit; previously exported reports are frozen artefacts, not silently rewritten. No arbitrary reporting lock period is invented.

## Counting people and scope

Active members means distinct workspace beneficiaries with effective qualifying active membership, not login accounts, plans, payers or visits. Guardian, trial, PT-only or staff roles alone do not count. One beneficiary with two memberships counts once company-wide. Cross-workspace identities never merge private records for a company total. Missing prior imported history means first-ever joiner status is unclassified rather than assumed new.

Gym membership reporting requires explicit attribution: home/enrolment-gym unique beneficiary distribution, or entitlement-gym distinct eligible beneficiaries. The latter overlaps for all-gym members and gym rows cannot be added to get the company distinct total. Display overlap explanation, distinct company result and selected basis. Recorded visit/attendance/PT/class metrics use actual service gym. Dependents are participants; a guardian booking/paying is not the attendee.

Conversion is a named submission cohort observed to a named cutoff. Applications and unique people are different denominators. Do not divide activations occurring this month by unrelated applications this month. Show cohort size, numerator, observation window and source quality. Retention flags use a published configured rule and known qualifying visit coverage; no visits is not proof somebody left or needs a medical intervention. AI suggestions remain reviewable, not automated personal judgement.

## Financial facts and signs

Charges, credits, incoming collections, refunds, reversals, allocations, supplier commitments, verified invoices and supplier cash are separate source facts. Receipts evidence issued transactions and do not add revenue again. A till collection for existing membership/PT debt points to the existing charge; no second retail sale/charge. Duplicate provider events/attempts are not extra movements. Verified external cash/manual events identify provenance; bank screenshot, DD mandate and provider request accepted are not confirmation. Actual provider states must be normalised into evidenced qualifying movements before money metrics are enabled.

Posted charges less posted credits is net charge activity, not cash collected or automatic recognised revenue. Net collections is incoming confirmed movement principal minus confirmed outgoing refunds and confirmed reversal principal over each event's own period. A refund request is not refund cash; a credit note changes debt without necessarily returning cash. Fees/tax are separately typed; no guessing whether provider payout net of fees equals gross customer payment. Payment-to-charge allocations explain settlement of debt; they are not additional incoming collections.

At a cutoff, remaining due uses valid charge/opening-debt principal less applicable credits and net retained allocations after recorded deallocation/refund/reversal effects. Do not both reverse payment allocation and add the reversal to debt as another charge. Credits beyond a charge's valid residual require explicit customer credit/unapplied handling; do not silently clamp corrupt over-allocation to zero. Separate valid customer funds/unapplied amounts and errors. Overdue requires known explicit due date; debt without a due date is unclassified, not automatically overdue.

Imported opening balances/history have explicit provenance: they can affect outstanding balances after review but are never new current-period collections. Restoring a backup or repeating import cannot create new charge/cash. Currency is mandatory; never sum different currencies or silently convert. If FX reporting is later configured, store rate/date/source and show native and converted values separately. Tax-inclusive/exclusive and rounding/basis are setup inputs. Operational movement amounts use recorded exact decimal/minor-unit representation; display rounding never rewrites source.

## Gym attribution and cost coverage

Money follows approved line-level company/gym allocations, preserving original legal issuer/currency. Allocation shares sum exactly to original amount; unallocated remainder is a visible bucket rather than an invented home gym. All-gym membership income is not copied in full into every entitled gym. Allocation edits version/restatement affect attribution without a new payment. Internal coach wage/share allocation is not customer revenue again; rental coach externally collected client money is outside gym revenue unless an actual configured gym issuer/merchant arrangement owns the transaction.

Supplier quote approval creates a commitment. When verified invoice lines consume that commitment, outstanding commitment decreases by linked value. Invoice verification creates recorded supplier invoice value/payable; later payment settles it, not a second expense. Shared invoice allocations sum to source total. Group/parent/child tasks and linked assets refer to the same cost lines: they do not each duplicate expense. Missing supplier/employee costs remain visible gaps, not zeros. Approved staffing costs are recorded imported/reviewed evidence, not invented payroll liability or statutory calculation.

Profit and margins are unavailable until accounting/service-income basis, tax treatment, required cost categories and attribution/completeness are reviewed. A permitted preliminary view is explicitly Known operating contribution with listed included/excluded costs; incomplete known subtotal cannot be labelled profit or used as unqualified gym ranking. Stock valuation requires an approved method and verified costs; units alone do not establish value. No tax rates, depreciation, valuation method or accounting basis are selected by this document.

## Service metrics and double-counting

Class bookings are unique participant-occurrence records; RSVP/waitlist/cancelled states excluded from confirmed booking capacity. Capacity is known published capacity for identical occurrence scope; missing capacity flags incomplete denominator. Reviewed attendance rate uses attended/(attended+reviewed no-show) and separately displays unresolved expected participants/register coverage; it is not the fraction of all booked people who attended. Walk-ins require actual register/eligibility records, not fabricated bookings. No-show requires reviewed source evidence, not a missed QR scan.

PT delivered hours and coach availability utilisation use matched service interval overlap, coach/gym scope and source quality. Availability intervals are unioned per coach; overlapping delivered appointments are flagged as conflict rather than silently capped at 100% or erased. Current coach rates never reprice past sales. Gym entry and class/PT delivery stay separate.

Task completion submission is distinct from management verification. Resubmissions show rework; parent/group rollups do not multiply source occurrences or cost lines. Count actionable task occurrences, exclude purely organisational group containers, and label whether parent/child records are shown. Historical verified outcomes remain recorded after reopening, alongside current reopened backlog. Asset downtime unions overlapping unavailable intervals per asset and clips to period/cutoff; job closure or paid invoice does not prove restored equipment. Current recorded occupancy may contain missed exits/stale records and is never a guaranteed evacuation count.

Stock metrics are per SKU/location/unit of measure. Receipt/dispatch/return/count adjustments have explicit signs and unique movement IDs. Refund is not a physical return. Transfer dispatch removes source sellable stock; receipt adds destination once; in-transit quantity is separate. Count variance uses pre-adjustment snapshot; posting the adjustment does not make the original discrepancy disappear. Mixed products/units and unknown cost bases cannot be fabricated into one meaningful valuation.

## Permissions, drilldown, exports and Orb

Owner sees own-company portfolio; platform admin uses audited explicit context; senior/local/specialist roles use current scoped grants and field bundles. Metric/report visibility and source drilldown are separately checked. A report permission is not unrestricted raw HR/health access. Aggregates require an explicitly permitted safe projection; do not leak hidden records through counts, chart labels, filters or errors. Do not grant broad underlying field permissions merely to enable a KPI. Payroll costs/disciplinary/health remain restricted; aggregates never expose questionnaire answers.

Clicking a metric opens its identical scoped predicate/snapshot/definition, with rows that reconcile to the displayed figure. Rate drilldown exposes numerator/denominator/exclusions; flow shows signed source movements; balance shows charges/credits/retained allocations. Filters survive Back and gym switch rechecks rights. If different raw-detail rights apply, state what is restricted instead of returning misleading unexplained totals. Exports retain report parameters/version/as-of/columns and current permission checks at job execution/download. Revocation stops future deliveries; already downloaded files cannot be remotely erased.

Orb uses the same metric registry/result snapshot, not an alternative prompt-invented formula. Each statement links metric ID/source result and notes incompleteness. AI cannot infer missing costs, claim causal fault or label revenue/profit without its basis. Suggested actions invoke existing source workflows and approvals. Scheduled reports remain an explicit release/configuration choice; viewing a report does not authorise recurring external mail.

## UI and setup

Reports supports gym/period/cutoff/status/source-quality filters, definition tooltip, refresh/as-of labels and accessible data table alternative for charts. Exact source currency and original values are available without clipped totals. No-data/zero/incomplete/stale/failed/denied/loading/export-pending/conflict states are separate. Default owner financial tile should use a precise chosen label such as Net collections rather than ambiguous Revenue this month; the generated concept figure is illustrative and not a definition. Dashboard Overview/Calendar/Feed structure remains unchanged.

Company reporting settings configures timezone, legal issuer/currency/tax basis, recorded financial category/status mapping, cost/gym allocation, retention/renewal horizons and any valuation/contribution basis. Keep existing source configuration authorities; this is no second payment or payroll engine. Missing values block dependent metrics while independent known metrics remain available. Preview changed definitions and impact; publish version, preserve historical definitions and document restatement. Target source freshness/refresh cadence is OpenKBS capacity/provider evidence, not an invented live dashboard promise.
