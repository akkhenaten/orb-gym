# Notification content and brand contract

Default brand is Orb Gym. Published company branding applies after explicit white-label activation; gym override only within established permitted inheritance. Use issued-document/receipt snapshot branding when referring to that record. Legal issuer and company logo are separate. Orb assistant name stays Orb. Auth provider branding can be constrained by the actual provider; inspect capability rather than promise unrestricted customisation.

External templates have purpose, source event ID, subject, short generic text, safe action label/link, optional permitted gym display and current sender identity. Ordinary push/email previews exclude health answers, private HR/pay values, raw quotes/invoices, gate credentials and other attendees. Attachments are protected record links by default, not emailed confidential files. Secure one-time identity links follow supported provider controls; never log their complete tokens.

| Context | Example copy | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Application | Your application is with the gym. Open your checklist to see whether anything else is needed. | Submitted, not approved/paid |
| Booking | Your class booking is confirmed. View your booking for the current details. | Send only after booking commit |
| Waitlist | A place has been offered to you. Open the app to check the current deadline and respond. | Offer, not confirmed reservation |
| Payment issue | There is an update requiring attention on your payment account. Sign in to review it. | No confidential amount/status in generic preview |
| Task | You have work requiring attention. Open the task for the current assignment and details. | Recipient access rechecked; no completion claim |
| Decision | A decision is waiting for your review. | Not already approved |
| Payslip | A payslip is available in your secure account. | No pay amount or attachment in external preview |
| Source changed | This item has changed. Review the latest details before acting. | No stale approval replay |
| Delivery unknown | The provider accepted this request, but delivery has not been confirmed. | Operator message, not recipient completion |

These are editable starting copy examples, not legal wording or an unrestricted promotion campaign. Templates use escaped allowlisted variables; uploaded content cannot inject commands or untrusted links. Missing values produce a safe fallback or blocked send with operator reason, not raw placeholders. Exact accessible HTML/plain text, provider lengths and localisation must be implemented/tested. External messages render brand with compatible readable fallback; they do not claim full app theme fidelity in every email client. In-app and public/auth screens retain the exact footer Orb Gym - powered by orbsolutions.co.uk; transactional external templates include readable platform attribution where supported, without overriding mandatory provider content.
