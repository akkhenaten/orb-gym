# Feature Options — Superseded by Completed Design 0.22

Following the instruction to fill the gaps, the four former proposals now have explicit optional/configurable product capabilities and feature journeys. The 0.21 proposal text is preserved at history/feature-options-v0.21.md. Do not use its “not in catalogue” wording as current scope.

- Trials/guests: [module](../modules/13-trials-and-guests.md) and [spec](../../spec/trials-guests.md).
- Juniors/guardians: [module](../modules/14-juniors-and-guardians.md) and [spec](../../spec/juniors-guardians.md).
- Import/reconciliation: [module](../modules/15-import-and-reconciliation.md) and [spec](../../spec/imports-reconciliation.md).
- Privileged authentication/recovery: [module](../modules/16-privileged-auth-and-recovery.md) and [spec](../../spec/privileged-auth-recovery.md).

These are documented capabilities, not evidence of an implemented app, active tenant offer, legal approval or real authentication change. Trial/junior services stay disabled until required company setup is complete. Actual provider, reviewed content and company values remain inputs under [setup readiness](../setup-and-readiness.md). Current operational defaults are in [specified rules](../decisions/operational-decisions.md).
