# Six selectable UI directions

Preset IDs are stable; company style choice applies consistently across all portals. Values in [theme-tokens.json](theme-tokens.json) complete a proposed baseline, with both modes resolved. Existing written product rules take precedence over generated concept imagery.

| ID | Distinct structural rules | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| basic-01 — Orb Gym Basic / Bento01 | Default; cobalt #044EFC decorative accent, cool grey canvas, white modular cards, 22px radius, soft restrained elevation; Inter Tight headings/Manrope body with fallbacks. Asymmetric bento only for overview cards; operational content retains readable form/table structure. | Arbitrary rainbow accents, bento tiles replacing a register or ledger. |
| flat-02 — Flat Studio | Solid planes, 0–4px corners, strong rules/bands, direct compact tables and no blur/shadow/gradient. Arial/system sans, clear column alignment. | Rounded floating bento panels or glass effects. |
| editorial-03 — Minimal Editorial | Warm neutral background, generous section spacing, serif display headings, sans controls, thin separators and card-free lists by default. Long-form content and calm hierarchy; tabular financial numerals. | Serif dense tables, decorative magazine layouts hiding operational actions. |
| glass-04 — Soft Glass | System sans, 20–24px grouped surfaces, 12–16px controls; transparency in decorative shell, dashboard summary frames and overlays. Forms/finance stay opaque. Reduced-transparency fallback is solid. | Blur behind essential text, low-contrast layered payments or private record previews. |
| console-05 — Performance Console | Dense labelled operational navigation, precise schedule/ledger grid, 4–8px corners, minimal shadows. Monospace only IDs/technical values; comfortable member/mobile density. | Monospace HR paragraphs, touch targets reduced by compact desktop mode. |

| neumorphic-06 — Neumorphic | Tonal surfaces, soft paired light/dark elevation, raised controls/icons and inset fields/tracks; explicit borders/focus and reduced-effects solid fallback. | Shadow-only states, unreadable embossing, modified original logos or depth replacing data hierarchy. |

See [the expanded Neumorphic, Glassmorphism and Flat rules and settings journey](ui-direction-settings.md). Five options are selectable; Neumorphic06 is recorded but deferred (founder decision, 9 Nov 2026). Glass includes controlled bokeh/frost on decorative outer surfaces; operational data stays opaque. Flat remains deliberately free of decorative depth.

Semantic danger/warning/success may use accessible state colours and always text/icons. Cobalt remains default decorative brand accent; a company's validated published palette may replace exposed mappings. Dark mode is a resolved theme, not CSS inversion. Logo master retains white backing until suitable approved reverse/icon assets exist. The footer and Orb assistant identity rules are invariant.

A complete change must update spacing, typography, shape, elevation, grouping, navigation surfaces and motion, as well as palette. Never change business states, entitlement or module availability with a style choice.
