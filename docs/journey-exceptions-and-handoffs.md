# Journey Exceptions and Handoff Rules

Repository 0.19. These branches expose already specified boundaries so happy-path summaries cannot imply automatic approval or completion. They do not choose values for unresolved commercial or safety policies. [Planning gaps](planning-gap-register.md) retain ownership; [trace map](journey-architecture-trace.md) supplies source/spec/acceptance links.

| Trigger / current context | Required response and next owner | Record/audience consequences |
|---|---|---|
| Signup retry, interrupted onboarding or existing email | Resume/link after verification, preserving intended company/gym/application. | No duplicate workspace/profile; staff/member invite does not create owner privileges. |
| PAR-Q/required agreement incomplete or needs review | Keep limited holding/PT onboarding state and route to the authorised reviewer. | A submitted form/signature is not fitness clearance; reception sees permitted restriction/instruction rather than full health answers. |
| DD method configured but no qualifying payment/approval | Keep financial setup, collection and activation states distinct; show resolution path to member/Membership staff. | Do not grant access merely from a mandate or uploaded evidence. Exact qualifying-payment/exception policy must be explicit. |
| Freeze, failed payment, cancellation or reinstatement | Apply configured effective billing/booking/access effects through the owning membership command. | Retain account login where required for resolution, historical receipts and agreements; no silent future-booking deletion. |
| Final class place, blocked regular date or waitlist offer | Resolve capacity/hold atomically, disclose unavailable occurrences and apply selected partial/expiry policy. | RSVP does not unexpectedly consume a place; avoid ambiguous all-series confirmation. |
| Payment late/uncertain after booking hold expires | Reconcile payment and capacity without a second charge or overselling; create the specified operational resolution. | Payment success alone cannot promise an already unavailable place. |
| Coach absence, room conflict, maintenance or staff departure | Flag affected sessions/shifts/tasks, identify responsible management and validate replacement availability/qualification. | Do not silently cancel every related class; deliberate changes notify affected current audiences. |
| Pass expired/revoked/invalid, repeated scan or missing checkout | Show truthful outcome, allow permitted lookup/correction/override and reconcile visit state. | Invite QR ≠ pass; static QR ≠ confirmed presence; repeated scans cannot toggle entry/exit. |
| Register incomplete or suspected no-show | Coach reviews and finalises attendance, with query/correction/waiver history. | Gym arrival ≠ class attendance; no-show consequences use accepted policy, not a casual note. |
| PT-only client requests ordinary member features | Present limited own PT tools or Membership upgrade route. | Retain one identity and PT history; full privileges wait for member activation conditions. |
| Rental arrangement changes to employed/another gym | Review effective assignment, private client access, future reservations and outstanding rent/earnings. | No automatic copying of the coach's independent client business into gym HR/member records. |
| Leave/swap rejected or cover unfilled | Retain approved published rota and show unresolved responsibility; review staff availability/conflicts. | A request is not a published shift change; scheduled duty is not physical presence. |
| Task blocked/partial or required child unfinished | Preserve per-item status/evidence and owner/next action; parent cannot appear fully complete. | Group/parent/dependency are different links; costs/evidence are not cloned on grouping. |
| Contractor declines or requests a quote variation | Management reassigns/clarifies or obtains a versioned authorised decision. | Assignment, delivery and acceptance differ; revised quote cannot silently inherit an inappropriate earlier approval. |
| Contractor submits completion/invoice | Manager verifies/returns work; finance matches authorised invoice/payment; asset service status separately reviewed. | Submission ≠ management closure, payment or certified service availability. |
| Tender failed/uncertain, refund or stock correction | Reconcile tender before recollection; record reasoned authorised reversal and separate return-to-stock. | One effective sale/stock movement; print/app/reprint never create new transaction identity. |
| Brand upload/draft or published rollback | Keep current published appearance until deliberate activation/publication; validate permitted inheritance. | Documents already issued/signed remain frozen. Theme selection cannot activate an unapproved logo or change privileges. |
| Public gym/coach edit invalid, private or archived | Keep last valid approved content or deliberate unpublish handling, preserving link/history policy. | No private guideline/HR/client leak or automatic platform marketing listing. |
| Member complaint/report or sensitive staff update | Use case/moderation owner and explicit audience; retain resolution/reopen/correction history. | Public reviews, member replies, vendor notes and internal HR discussion never merge by reused feed UI. |
| Role revoked, session expired or deep link stale | Reauthorise before read/submit/export/AI/background effect; give permitted next step. | Old notification/link is not a persistent grant; show local draft versus durable save truthfully. |
| Orb/provider/background work fails or repeats | Return pending/failed/partial status, preserve source/audit and use deduplicated authorised retry. | AI output cannot invent approvals, signatures, payment success or task verification. Normal tools remain usable. |

The builder must translate these rules and GAP01–GAP16 into exact actor/state/event/notification tables. Open values and alternatives remain visibly unresolved; they cannot be converted into guessed defaults by implementing a UI.
