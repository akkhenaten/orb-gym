# Operational Decisions for Discussion

Edition 0.21 · 7 November 2026. **Every recommendation below is PROPOSED, not approved policy.** The listed GAP categories remain governing context. These choices block only their affected workflow automation/feature, not unrelated planning. The builder must record an owner answer before implementing the chosen rule. No reply, elapsed time or creation of this file counts as approval.

| ID / related gaps | Question and consequence | Recommendation and alternatives | Affected work |
|---|---|---|---|
| DEC-GR-01 / GAP09, GAP13 | Who acts when a decision owner leaves, is absent or cannot approve? An unowned approval can strand cleaning/remedial/admin/contractor work. | PROPOSED: configurable backup approver within existing grants, with owner escalation when no suitable delegate exists. Alternatives: owner-only fallback or a defined approval group. Do not auto-approve, broaden roles or allow contractor self-approval. Timing, limits and conflict rules require decision. | WF-TASK-18, related staff leaver/approval workflows; AC61 |
| DEC-GR-02 / GAP02–03, GAP14 | What happens if an activated member's collection is later reversed or fails? What happens to access, future classes, notices and coach allocations? | PROPOSED: open an owned finance/Membership case and apply an explicit configurable arrears/grace policy. Alternatives include immediate restriction or manager review before restriction. No grace duration, retry charge or automatic cancellation is approved. Keep payment evidence/history and account resolution access distinct. | WF-MEM-08; AC56 |
| DEC-GR-03 / GAP03, GAP11 | Which existing members must accept a new form/policy, and what rights remain while review is outstanding? | PROPOSED: versioned targeted review with applicability/effective date and an explicit chosen entitlement effect. Alternatives: require at renewal, require before specified activity, or review before a defined restriction. Do not assume an annual PAR-Q cycle or infer fitness. | WF-MEM-09; AC57 |
| DEC-GR-04 / GAP05–06, GAP09, GAP13 | For coach absence, failed equipment or paid-class disruption, who decides replacement, reschedule, cancellation, member response and refund/credit? | PROPOSED: named manager owns a per-occurrence resolution with validated cover and affected booking/financial review. Determine when member acceptance is required, financial treatment and announcement timing. Do not silently substitute service or duplicate bookings/refunds. | WF-CLASS-07, WF-STAFF-16; AC58/60 |
| DEC-GR-05 / GAP03, GAP14–16 | What may a gym archive do to single/all-gym rights, future classes/PT, staff, assets/stock and money? | PROPOSED: owner impact review and domain-specific resolution/assigned follow-up before the permitted closure action. Transfer, cancellation/refund and access outcomes require explicit business policy; no automatic contract/staff transfer. | WF-SPACE-05; AC66 |
| DEC-GR-06 / GAP02, GAP08, GAP14 | What should staff do when a tender is confirmed but sale/stock allocation fails, or a payment cannot be matched? | PROPOSED: recover the original operation into an owned reconciliation queue; authorised fulfilment or refund/other remedy. Decide cash/till rights, substitution/refund/overpayment handling and accounting basis. Do not charge again merely because a device/UI timed out. | WF-POS-09, WF-FIN-05; AC67–68 |
| DEC-GR-07 / GAP02, GAP05, GAP07, GAP14 | Does the independent rental coach collect class fees, or does the gym collect and account for an agreed fee/share? | PROPOSED: explicit per-arrangement/class mode and honest public terms, implementing only the chosen supported model. Outside-app coach collection and gym-collected services have different issuer, refund and settlement responsibilities. No payout product/provider or payroll split is assumed. | WF-COACH-07; AC69 |

## Optional feature scope decisions

The four proposals in [feature options](../docs/proposals/feature-options.md) are **not** added to the approved feature/workflow catalogue. Decide whether they belong in the first release, a later release or remain out of scope: trial/guest access; junior/guardian accounts; legacy data import; privileged multi-factor/recovery controls. The original hourly PT scope and exclusion of prepaid PT packages remain unchanged.

## How an answer propagates

Record answer/date/role/scope and effective rule → update governing decision and relevant workflow guards → update owning spec/permissions/UI/notification/reporting → link actual numbered delivery cards → update local/cross-module acceptance → later attach executed evidence. Preserve rejected alternatives and previous versions. A recommendation must never become approved because it appears in a plan.
